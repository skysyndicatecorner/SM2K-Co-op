**SM2K Co-op v0.12** is the first public release. Play Spider-Man (2000, PC) with a friend on one PC
(split-screen) or on two PCs (LAN / Radmin VPN).

> 🇷🇺 Первый публичный релиз. Spider-Man (2000, PC) вдвоём: на одном ПК (разделённый экран) или на двух
> (LAN / Radmin VPN). Инструкция на русском лежит в архиве (`README-RU.txt`) и в [README.ru.md](https://github.com/{{REPO}}/blob/main/README.ru.md).

### Download
`sm2k-coop-v0.12-win32.zip` → put `dinput8.dll` next to `SpideyPC.exe` (game v1.3), start a level, press **F6**.

### What's in it
- **Split-screen co-op:** player 2 has his own camera, the game's own HUD, his own health and web between
  levels, and keyboard or XInput gamepad controls. There is a downed state: player 1 holds Num0 next to him to
  revive him.
- **New: LAN co-op, phase 1.** Each player plays on his own PC and sees the partner. Enemy deaths, trigger zones
  and level changes are synced. Set it up in F6 → `LAN CO-OP`.

### Known limitations
- In LAN mode each PC fights its own enemies; only deaths are shared. Some enemy types don't die over the network.
- The partner can't be hurt over the network yet, and he wears your costume.

### Verify
The DLL is built by GitHub Actions with `scripts/build-linux-clang.sh` and is reproducible.
Compare its hash with `SHA256SUMS.txt`, or run `gh attestation verify`.
If your antivirus flags it, see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/{{REPO}}/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

### Please report
Open an issue with `coop_log.txt` (from **both** PCs for LAN problems).
